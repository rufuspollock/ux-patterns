## What makes it good

- **Purpose first.** One plain sentence on what the research is for.
- **Areas row.** Each research area with an icon, a post count and one line.
- **Every item has art** from one illustration system, so a long list stays inviting.
- **Just enough per item:** area, date, title, two lines of summary.
