The reference stylesheet for essays: sidenotes, margin notes, margin figures and full-width figures in plain HTML and CSS.

https://edwardtufte.github.io/tufte-css/

![Tufte CSS screenshot](https://screenshotit.app/https://edwardtufte.github.io/tufte-css/)

## What makes it good

- **Sidenotes and margin notes** without JavaScript, collapsing to toggles on phones.
- **Three figure widths:** margin, main column, full width.
- **Measure.** A text column of about 55% of the page, ET Book serif, off-white paper.
- **Epigraphs and new-thought small caps** for openings.

The page demonstrates itself. Most of the sidenote patterns elsewhere in this collection trace back here.
