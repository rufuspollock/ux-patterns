## What makes it good

- **Sidenotes.** Notes in the margin on desktop, beside the line they gloss.
- **Popups.** Hover a link to preview the target, including other essays and papers.
- **Metadata up top.** Status, confidence, created and modified dates under an italic abstract.
- **Contents box** floated beside the opening.

Dense and a little forbidding: the best model for notes and contents, less so for an inviting opening.
