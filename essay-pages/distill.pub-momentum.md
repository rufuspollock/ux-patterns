The model research article for the web: interactive figures in the text, a byline block, footnotes and a full citation apparatus.

![Distill · Why Momentum Really Works screenshot](https://screenshotit.app/https://distill.pub/2017/momentum/)

## What makes it good

- **Figures that explain.** Interactive diagrams sit in the column and break wider where they need room.
- **Byline block.** Authors, affiliations, dates and DOI in a tidy grid under the title.
- **Scholarly end matter.** Footnotes, references, a BibTeX citation, updates and corrections.
- **Measure.** One column of about 70 characters, serif text, generous margins for asides.

Distill is archived but still the benchmark. The site itself is already noted in [Docs and Explainer Sites](/docs-and-explainer-sites/distill.pub).
