# Essay and Paper Pages

How to make great pages for long-form essays, research papers and reports on the web: the long read itself, a paper's own page, and the index that holds a body of work.

Started 2026-10-23. Several items and notes come from the [SoR Papers moodboard](https://claude.ai/artifact/YUCyzZ1MotbHAV7rzj6rb4) made for the Seeds of Renaissance papers. Related: [Docs and Explainer Sites](/docs-and-explainer-sites/), [Report Inspirations](/report-inspirations/), [Research Sites](/research-sites/), [Typography](/typography).

## What makes a great essay page

- **Measure.** One column of about 60–75 characters; serif or a warm grotesque at 18–21px.
- **The opening.** Decide how much image: art first (Emergence), art beside the title (Noema), photo under the title (Aeon), or none (Gwern). Get title, summary and the PDF on the first screen.
- **The claim up front.** A summary or standfirst, with the key sentence findable at a glance (Anthropic sets it in bold).
- **Contents in reach.** A sticky sidebar or a slim chapter bar for anything over a few thousand words.
- **Notes beside the line.** Sidenotes in the margin on desktop, at section end or toggles on phones (Tufte CSS, Gwern).
- **Figures in three widths:** margin, column, full width. Figures that explain, not decorate (Distill, Bret Victor).
- **Metadata out of the reading line.** Authors, date, status and team in a side column or a compact block.
- **Where it sits.** For a series, show the parts and this one's place (Situational Awareness, Noema's series box).
- **Scholarly end matter.** References, cite-as, version and licence (arXiv is the floor; Distill does it beautifully).
- **One image system** so every piece has its own art and still reads as one house (Anthropic, Works in Progress).
