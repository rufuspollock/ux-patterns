## What makes it good

- **The paper is the page.** Title, art, summary, then the full text. No separate landing page to click through.
- **Claim in bold.** The key sentence of the summary is set bold, so the finding is findable in a second.
- **Chapter bar.** A slim bar at the top shows the current section and opens the contents: navigation for a long read in one line.
- **Metadata out of the reading line.** Date and team sit in a side column.
- **One illustration system.** Archival engravings and photos cut with flat colour blocks give every piece its own image while reading as one house.
