A body of essays as a book's contents: each part a wide band with an engraved plate, a paragraph on what it argues, and its essays.

https://www.lesswrong.com/rationality

![LessWrong · Rationality A–Z screenshot](https://screenshotit.app/https://www.lesswrong.com/rationality)

## What makes it good

- **The whole as one argument.** Each book says what it argues before listing its parts.
- **Engraved plates** give each band an image without photos.
- **Calm, bookish type.**
