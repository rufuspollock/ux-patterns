A guided path: each part is a question with a short answer, plus a one-minute summary and the same guide as book and audio.

![80,000 Hours · Career guide screenshot](https://screenshotit.app/https://80000hours.org/career-guide/)

## What makes it good

- **Question-and-answer parts.** Each chapter card states its question and answers it in a line.
- **One-minute summary** of the whole for people who won't read it all.
- **Many formats:** web, book, audio, podcast.

The idea is worth taking; the look is too corporate.
