## Layout

H1, italic venue and date, summary as dek. One narrow column, no subheads; short standalone lines act as breaks. Dinkus, related, postscript, sign-off.

## Style

Equity Text with Alix and Perc. The root size steps down in small increments across many breakpoints, so the measure holds at every width.

## Steal

Stepped fluid sizing. The dinkus before end matter.
