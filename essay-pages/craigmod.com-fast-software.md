## What makes it good

- **Book rhythm on screen.** Large text in a humanist sans (FF Meta), a comfortable measure, real paragraph rhythm.
- **No chrome.** No sidebar, no popups; the author's small portrait sits at the foot of the essay.
- **Pure text.** Proof that a long essay needs no images if the type is good.
