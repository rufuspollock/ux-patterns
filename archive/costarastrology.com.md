Elegant, minimalist black-and-white astrology app site — monospace type, arty grayscale photography, and a sciencey edge.

https://www.costarastrology.com

![Co-Star screenshot](https://screenshotit.app/https://www.costarastrology.com/@full)

Clean, minimal, monospace throughout — feels almost like a terminal (the "I was born in ___ on ___" sign-up field even reads like a CLI prompt). Combines art and science: grainy grayscale photography (moon, hand, skull) sits next to technical birth-chart and transit-wheel diagrams, and the copy leans on "NASA data" and "algorithmically generate" language rather than mystical framing. Modest, restrained palette (pure black/white, no color) with the astrology charts as the main graphic interest. Beautifully designed overall — a rare case where a very cold/technical aesthetic still feels warm and considered.
