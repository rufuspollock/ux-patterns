# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People exploring Ken Wilber and integral theory who value concise orientation,
trustworthy source distinctions, and visually memorable explanations. Individual
surfaces may serve different moments: browsing the catalog, understanding the
development of the ideas, or sharing an explanatory artifact with a wider audience.

## Product Purpose

Wilber Wiki is a concept-level knowledge base that makes Wilber's works, concepts,
intellectual development, and interlocutors easier to explore. Success means readers
can orient themselves without flattening uncertain history or mistaking editorial
interpretation for documented fact.

## Positioning

The site combines a verified chronological works catalog and source-grounded excerpts
with selective interpretive guides. It distinguishes bibliographic facts, documented
relationships, and editorial synthesis instead of presenting them as equally certain.

## Operating Context

The public site is built from Markdown with Flowershow. Its works area includes a
40-entry chronological catalog, individual work pages, a selective reading guide, and
a Wilber I–V intellectual-history guide. Visual artifacts should work on the web and,
when appropriate, as legible screenshots or exported images for social sharing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the chronological works catalog as the complete reference inventory.
- Treat a simple all-books timeline, an intellectual-history overview, and a dense
  relationship map as distinct artifacts rather than one overloaded interface.
- Phase boundaries are approximate and may overlap.
- Relationship types include retitling, revision, abridgment, anthology extraction,
  and conceptual development or application.
- Documentary relationships and editorial inferences must be visually distinct and
  sourced; unknown revision extent remains explicit.
- Publishing is a separate action governed by `AGENTS.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

- `works/index.md`: chronological catalog and cover imagery.
- `works/phases.md`: sourced Wilber I–V and Kosmos trilogy overview.
- `works/reading-guide.md`: editorial reading routes and relationship summaries.
- `docs/plans/2026-09-14-works-bibliography-audit.md`: 40-entry evidence ledger.
- `assets/covers/`: 28 cached book-cover images with provenance records.

## Product Principles

- Make complexity inviting without pretending it is simple.
- Let evidence and uncertainty remain visible.
- Preserve the catalog while adding complementary ways to see the whole.
- Prefer one strong explanatory idea per artifact over an all-purpose visualization.
- Design shareable visuals that reward a second, closer look; do not promise reach.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive visuals require keyboard access, non-color-only encoding, readable labels,
and a text alternative. Static exports must remain legible at their intended size.
